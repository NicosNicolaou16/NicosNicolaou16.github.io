01About

Hello 👋 — I'm Nicos, based in Nicosia, Cyprus. I hold a BSc in Computer Science and an MSc in Mobile Systems, and I work as a Senior Software Engineer specialising in Android and Flutter.

I published my first Android app on the Play Store back in 2016 — UrLocFinder. Since 2020 I've focused on open source, exploring new Google technologies across more than forty public and private repositories.

From May 2023 I began publishing my own libraries and packages for Android Native and Flutter, and since November 2023 I've written regularly on Medium about the latest mobile tooling.

Away from the keyboard you'll find me playing guitar, building PCs, and getting far too invested in hardware benchmarks and PC games.
